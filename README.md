My Little Pony: The Midnight Infection








knowyourmeme.com


Game Title

My Little Pony: The Midnight Infection (working title).

Main Goal

Play as a customizable pony, investigate Applejack's disappearance, discover the origin of a mysterious infection, find a cure, and escape the infected castle.

Player Controls

On-screen directional buttons to move around the environment. Additional buttons will activate magical abilities and interact with objects.

Gameplay

The game follows a narrative divided into several chapters:
1. The Missing Apple: Attend a picnic with the Mane 6 and discover Applejack is missing.
2. The Midnight Summons: Follow a mysterious blue light from Ponyville toward Canterlot.
3. The Infection: Encounter infected ponies and discover the emergency at the castle.
4. The Investigation: Learn about the fruit bats, infected apples, and the origin of the virus.
5. The Cure: Search for ingredients and discover a formula while trying to protect your remaining friends.
6. The Escape: Survive a castle lockdown and escape during a dangerous chase.
Scoring

Earn points by collecting clues, discovering ingredients, finding potions, and completing objectives.

Win/Lose Rules
- Win by discovering the cure and successfully escaping the castle.
- Lose when the player's health reaches zero or they fail a critical survival challenge.
First Version

A static browser game using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, featuring:
- A custom pony character and name.
- Story dialogue and multiple scenes.
- On-screen movement controls.
- Collectible potions and temporary abilities.
- A health system.
- Simple infected-creature movement.
- Investigation and collection objectives.
- A final escape sequence.
